Operational note

This is a situational-awareness aid, not a destination-control or medical-triage tool. State directories may be incomplete or delayed. Missouri ED status is sourced from DHSS; Illinois licensed-hospital records are candidates and are not independently proof of an operating ED. Confirm ED status, capability, trauma designation, diversion status and destination with medical control and current EMS protocols. Do not delay emergency care to use this app.

Driving estimates are ordinary road-routing estimates, not ambulance response or transport times.
